April 2026

Invited Lecture Tour in China & Hong Kong

Professor Stergios Chatzikyriakidis completed a prestigious invited lecture tour across leading universities in China and Hong Kong, presenting cutting-edge research on large language models, formal semantics, and neural-symbolic platform architectures:

  • East China Normal University (Shanghai, Apr 1): "Computational Linguistics/NLP and Theoretical Linguistics: Two Worlds Apart?"
  • Beijing Normal University (Beijing, Apr 8): "An LLM-powered Neural-Symbolic Platform for Classical Languages"
  • Center of Greek and Chinese Ancient Civilizations (KELKIP, Chongqing, Apr 10): "MEDEA-NEUMOUSA: An LLM-powered Neural-Symbolic Platform for Classical Languages"
  • Division of Intercultural Communication (Shenzhen, Apr 13): "Formal Semantics and Large Language Models"
  • The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (Hong Kong, Apr 16): Presented two lectures: "Neural-Symbolic NLP: A Dual Taxonomy and Two Case Studies in Poetry Generation and Knowledge Extraction" and "Using Perplexity to Measure Dialectal Distance: A Distance Study of Modern Greek Dialects".

November 2025

GRDD+: Extended Greek Dialectal Dataset Released

Extended version of GRDD with 10 Greek dialectal varieties including Cretan, Cypriot, Pontic, Northern Greek, Greco-Corsican, Griko, Maniot, Heptanesian, Tsakonian, and Katharevusa. Total size: 6.4M words with fine-tuning evaluation across multiple LLM architectures.
