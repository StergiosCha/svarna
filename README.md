# SVARNA

**Semantics, Variation And Reasoning in Neural-symbolic Architectures**

Independent computational linguistics lab led by Prof. Stergios Chatzikyriakidis — Athens.
Neuro-symbolic NLP, formal semantics, Greek language varieties, and the computational study of texts.

A substantial part of the research showcased here was carried out together with the CLLT lab
(Computational Linguistics and Language Technology Lab) at the University of Crete, which
Prof. Chatzikyriakidis founded and previously directed. As of 2026, the two labs are separate
and independent.
